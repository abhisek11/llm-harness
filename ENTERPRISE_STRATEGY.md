# Topi Harness: Enterprise Strategy & Monetization

While Topi Harness is an open-source transparent proxy, it is designed with an "Open-Core" business model in mind. This document outlines why enterprises will pay for this software and the typical pricing models used in the AI Gateway / Context Compression market.

## Why Enterprises Pay for Open Source

1. **Fully Managed Cloud (SaaS)**: Enterprises lack the time and resources to deploy, maintain, and scale proxy infrastructure globally. They pay for a hosted version with 99.99% uptime guarantees.
2. **Security & Compliance**: Large corporations require features absent in the free tier, such as Okta/SAML SSO, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and SOC2/HIPAA compliant audit logging.
3. **Support & SLAs**: Enterprises need 24/7 dedicated support and a direct line to engineers when mission-critical AI workflows break.
4. **Proprietary Optimizations**: While the open-source version might offer standard context compression (e.g., 40% savings), the enterprise tier could include proprietary models that safely compress data by 80% without performance loss.
5. **On-Premises Deployment**: Regulated industries (banking, healthcare) pay a premium to deploy the software entirely within their own private networks (VPCs) to ensure data never leaves their infrastructure.

## Typical Enterprise Pricing Models

- **Value-Based (ROI)**: If Topi Harness saves an enterprise $1M/year in OpenAI API costs through context compression, a $150,000/year enterprise license is a highly justifiable expense.
- **Usage-Based**: Charging by the volume of tokens or requests routed through the proxy (e.g., $1,000 - $3,000/month for mid-market, scaling to $50,000+/year for heavy usage).
- **Seat-Based**: If tied to internal developer tools, vendors typically charge $20 to $50 per user/month. For a 1,000-person engineering team, this equates to $240,000 - $600,000 annually.
- **On-Prem Premium**: Flat-rate enterprise licenses for on-premises deployment typically start at $50,000 to $100,000 per year minimum.
